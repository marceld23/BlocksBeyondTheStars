HOW TO PLAY

Click the game view once to capture your mouse (Esc releases it again). Keyboard + mouse, Xbox-style gamepads and touch (tablets, phones) all work - and every key can be rebound in Settings > Controls.

ON FOOT
WASD move · Space jump · Ctrl crouch · Mouse look · Left click mine · Right click place · Middle click slot actions (colour, shape, rotate) · 1-9 hotbar · E use/interact · F attack · G loot · H deposit into crate · R repair / rotate shape · L lamp · V change view · M map · T trade with a player · Tab menu · Enter chat

SPACE FLIGHT
WASD + mouse fly · 1-9 or mouse wheel select ship system · Left click use system · E land/dock · L back to your launch pad · M star chart and waypoint · V change view · F leave the helm and walk around inside your ship (use the airlock for a spacewalk)

SPACEWALK (EVA)
WASD + mouse float · Space/Ctrl up and down · B deploy a station core · float back to your ship and press E to get in

GETTING STARTED
Follow VEGA, your ship's AI - it shows newcomers around: mine a few resources, craft your first tools, then launch into orbit and pick a new planet to explore. Build a base, grow food, tame creatures - the whole galaxy is block-built and yours to reshape. Open "What's new?" in the main menu to see the latest updates.

MULTIPLAYER
In the shared arcade worlds you play with others: be friendly, build cool stuff, trade with T, and use /report on players who misbehave.

Playing on a tablet or phone? Touch controls appear automatically. Found a bug or have an idea? Press F2 in the browser (F1 in the desktop game) to send feedback.
